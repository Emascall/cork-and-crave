About your host

Meet Emma Grafton.

Founder & Wine Pairing Host, Cork & Crave · Austin, Texas

I’m a certified sommelier and a culinary arts student at Austin Community College (ACC), with a love for making wine feel welcoming, memorable and genuinely fun.

I started Cork & Crave because traditional wine tastings can feel intimidating, while the most memorable food experiences are often relaxed, social and rooted in discovery. My goal is to give guests the confidence to explore wine through familiar snacks, chef-inspired bites and clear explanations of why flavors work together.

Each tasting combines culinary technique with warm hospitality. I build the menu around the occasion, the guests and the flavors they already enjoy—then add a few delicious surprises.

Whether you know your Burgundy from your Bordeaux or simply know what tastes good, you’ll leave with new favorites and a better understanding of why each pairing works.
